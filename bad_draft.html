Taiwan Housing Intelligence Agent: A Spec-Driven AI System for Traceable Market Analysis

Abstract—Taiwan's housing market presents significant decision-making challenges for buyers seeking reliable property comparisons in an increasingly complex transaction landscape. Public housing transaction records, while extensive, do not represent the full market; variations in property types, area measurement standards, and parking definitions significantly affect price comparisons. We propose a spec-driven AI system for market analysis. We evaluate the proposed system on official transaction records. Preliminary outcomes indicate transparency improvements.

I. Introduction

Real estate acquisitions constitute one of the most substantial financial investments individuals undertake. In Taiwan, the Ministry of the Interior operates the Actual Price Registration (APR) platform to record property conveyances.

However, users face significant obstacles when comparing heterogeneous properties due to uncalibrated parking inclusions and variable balcony square meters.

II. Proposed Scheme

The proposed framework incorporates a conversational interface linked to a database query engine. When an inquiry is submitted, an agent executes SQL statements over property listings.

III. Evaluation

We evaluate transaction queries on a snapshot from Taichung City. Preliminary queries succeed in retrieving records within 1.2 seconds.
