# Abstract

Modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs) confront an overwhelming volume of security alerts, of which only a small fraction represent authentic malicious intrusions. Official empirical telemetry reveals that Taiwan's critical infrastructure was targeted by 2.63 million daily cyber intrusion probes in 2025 (+6% YoY) [NSB 2026], while the IBM Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026 recorded an all-time peak average breach cost of $4.99 million (+12% YoY) with a 247-day containment lifecycle [IBM 2026]. Concurrently, Mandiant M-Trends 2026 reported a global median attacker dwell time of only 14 days [Mandiant 2026], underscoring the criticality of rapid triage. However, in real-world enterprise telemetry (Microsoft GUIDE), True Positives constitute only 20.6% of incidents, whereas Benign Positives (49.0%) and False Positives (30.4%) dominate, causing acute analyst alert fatigue. Crucially, empirical evaluations demonstrate that five mainstream tool-using LLM agent strategies suffer a false negative rate of at least 40.4% due to premature investigation termination when log queries return empty sets, while ExCyTIn-Bench threat investigation rewards plateau at 0.606. To resolve these challenges, we propose the **AI SOC Analyst Agent**: a spec-driven, multi-tier system uniting calibrated Gradient Boosted Decision Tree (GBDT) classification, MITRE ATT&CK STIX 2.1 knowledge retrieval, and bounded ReAct planning with an explicit Evidence Gate preventing premature closure. The architecture is decomposed into eight functional modules governed by an RFC 2119 behavioral specification. Evaluated on Microsoft GUIDE (~1M incidents) and ExCyTIn-Bench under GroupKFold partitioning to prevent cross-tenant data leakage, our calibrated escalation reduces the 40.4% LLM miss rate to 6.8%, achieves a Macro-F1 of 0.895, and maintains an 18.2% human escalation rate, quantitatively tracing an actionable Pareto frontier between automation efficiency and analyst oversight.

> **Index Terms:** Security Operations Center, Alert Triage, LLM Agent, MITRE ATT&CK, Explainable AI, Human-in-the-Loop, Spec-Driven Development.
